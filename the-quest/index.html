Because of Jesus, we can live with Heavenly Father and with our families forever if we keep the commandments.

  1. Spin, then tap your piece (or the glowing space) to hop one space at a time.
  2. Landmarks. The 13 numbered landmarks are the foundation from The Quest. Everyone stops at each one and takes its challenge. Pass the challenge to add that stone to your foundation. Then keep hopping with any moves you have left.
  3. Milestones like Baptism and the Resurrection also pause your move so you can read them.
  4. Storms are pride, anger, jealousy, fear, greed, hatred and arrogance. If your stones are at least the storm’s strength, your house stands. After baptism, the Holy Ghost adds 1.
  5. Missed a stone? Choose the Right spaces later on the trail can win it back.
  6. Collect blessings ★ along the way. When everyone is home, the winner has the most points: blessings + 2 for each stone + 1 for each storm overcome.
