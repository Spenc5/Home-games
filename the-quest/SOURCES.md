# Sources

Every quotation, scripture and song title in The Quest comes from churchofjesuschrist.org (lds.org). Each quotation was checked word for word against these pages.

## The Quest handout

The 13 landmarks are the foundation list from the handout “The Quest,” in the handout’s order. The storms are the “plagues of the world” the handout lists. These come from President Russell M. Nelson’s talk “Overcome the World and Find Rest,” October 2022 general conference. The closing quotation on the final screen is from the same talk:
https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/general-conference/2022/10/47nelson?lang=eng

## Written for the game

The game instructions, the modern Choose the Right situations, the short scripture-story setups, and the storm descriptions are written for this game in plain language for children. They summarize no doctrine. Every doctrinal statement in the game is a direct quotation listed above.
