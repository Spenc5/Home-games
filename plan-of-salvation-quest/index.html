Plan of Salvation Quest

Who is playing?

Up to four players. Pass the phone or sit around the computer.

Overcome the world

“My plea to you this morning is to find rest from the intensity, uncertainty, and anguish of this world by overcoming the world through your covenants with God.” President Russell M. Nelson, “Overcome the World and Find Rest,” October 2022 general conference
  1. Spin and move along the plan: premortal life, earth life, the spirit world, the Resurrection, and home.
  2. Stop at every gold milestone, like Baptism and the Temple, even if you have moves left.
  3. Build your house on the rock. Collect the 13 foundation stones (prayer, scriptures, tithing and more).
  4. Storms are the “plagues of the world” like pride, anger and fear. If your foundation is strong enough, your house stands.
  5. Collect blessings ★ from scriptures, good choices, service, songs and the Sabbath.
  6. Everyone makes it home. The player with the most points wins: blessings, plus 2 for each stone, plus 1 for each storm overcome.

Everyone made it home!

“For behold, this is my work and my glory—to bring to pass the immortality and eternal life of man.” Moses 1:39
