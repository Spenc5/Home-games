# Regulation Bingo

A bingo game for practicing self-regulation strategies, made for one-on-one social skills sessions over Zoom. Each square is a strategy. The student reads the dice, finds the square, shows the strategy on camera, and places their own token. Each bingo earns a new accessory for their buddy.

## Playing

Open `index.html` in a web browser and share your screen. It's one file with nothing to install. You can click everything, or give the student remote control.

1. **Who's playing?** Add a student and pick their buddy (cat, dog, bunny, bear, frog, fox, panda or owl). Students, buddies and accessories are saved in this browser on this computer.
2. **Make a card.** Choose:
   - **Card:** Move & Stretch (animal yoga and movement), Calm & Think (breathing and thinking tools), or Mixed.
   - **Energy:** a mix, mostly calming 🌙, or mostly waking up ⚡.
   - **Size:** 3 × 3 (quick), 4 × 4, or 5 × 5 (free space in the middle).
   - **Token:** an animal, a shape in any color, or a nature or fun picture.
   Each card is dealt at random from about 40 strategies per deck, so it's different every time.
3. **Pop the bubble.** The **gold die** is the row and the **blue die** is the column. Nothing lights up: the student reads the dice and taps that square.
   - Wrong square? They get "Not quite!" After 2 misses, the row and column numbers light up as a hint.
   - If the square is already covered, the open squares in that row and column turn green and the student picks one. If both are full, pop again.
   - Before popping, tapping a covered square shows what was done there.
4. **Show it.** The card has a picture, simple steps, a **Read aloud** button, and for many strategies a breathing guide, hold timer or counter. When the student has shown the strategy, they tap **I did it!**, then tap their square to place the token.
   Covered squares fill in with the card's color, with a big token on a white circle.
5. **Bingo!** Any row, column, diagonal or the four corners wins (four corners doesn't count on 3 × 3). The student picks **1 new accessory** per bingo and **3** for a blackout. Accessories stay with the student, and they can change what their buddy wears any time with **Dress up**.

There are 26 accessories: hats, glasses, things for the neck, capes and wings, things to hold, and backgrounds.

## Make my own card

On the home screen, **✏️ Make my own card** lets the student build a card themselves:

- Pick a size (3 × 3, 4 × 4, or 5 × 5 with a free space).
- Tap strategies from **Move & Stretch** or **Calm & Think** to put them on the card. Tap a square on the card to take it off, or to choose which square the next one goes in.
- Under **My own**, tap **Write my own** to add a new strategy: a name, a picture, whether it's moving or calming/thinking, whether it calms down or wakes up, and optional steps. With no steps, its card says "Show how you do it!"
- **Fill the rest for me** fills any empty squares at random. **Play my card** starts the game with the squares where the student put them.

The card is saved for that student, so they can come back and play it again. Strategies students write are listed under **Strategies → Made by students**, where you can edit or delete them.

## Strategies

Every strategy is tagged ⚡ **Wake up**, 🌙 **Calm down**, or both.

- **Move & Stretch** includes the Forest Animals yoga poses (Porcupine, Bear, Owl, Rabbit, Frog, Snake, Chipmunk, Woodpecker in a Tree, Skunk, Raccoon), more animal poses (Flamingo, Cat and Cow, Puppy Dog, Butterfly, Turtle Shell, Lion's Breath, Crab Walk and more), and movement such as wall push-ups, jumping jacks and cross crawls.
- **Calm & Think** includes breathing (Smell the Flower, Balloon Belly, Square Breathing, Bumble Bee Hum and more), grounding (5-4-3-2-1, Listen for 3 Sounds), and thinking tools: Size of the Problem, Name It to Tame It, End of the Rainbow (thinking about how you'll feel, or what you'll get to do, when the work is done), What's Ahead (steps still to come), Make a Plan, Kind Words to Myself and more.

**Strategies** on the home screen lets you:
- **Star** a strategy for a student, so it's always on their card (good for IEP goals or favorites).
- **Edit** the name, steps or wake-up/calm-down tag. Edits are saved in this browser.

**What's Ahead** has no steps yet, so it stays off the cards until steps are added (under Strategies, or in `STRATS`).

To change the strategies for everyone, edit `STRATS` near the top of the script in `index.html`. The pose drawings are in `POSE`.

The pose pictures are original drawings. The Forest Animals pose names come from a Tools to Grow card set; their pictures are not copied here.

Turn sound effects off with **Sound** at the top.
