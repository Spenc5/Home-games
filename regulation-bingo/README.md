# Regulation Bingo

A bingo game for practicing self-regulation strategies, made for one-on-one social skills sessions over Zoom. Each square is a strategy. The student shows it on camera to cover the square. Each bingo earns a new accessory for their buddy.

## Playing

Open `index.html` in a web browser and share your screen. It's one file with nothing to install. You can click everything, or give the student remote control.

1. **Who's playing?** Add a student and pick their buddy (cat, dog, bunny, bear, frog, fox, panda or owl). Students, buddies and accessories are saved in this browser on this computer.
2. **Make a card.** Choose:
   - **Card:** Move & Stretch (animal yoga and movement), Calm & Think (breathing and thinking tools), or Mixed.
   - **Energy:** a mix, mostly calming 🌙, or mostly waking up ⚡.
   - **Size:** 3 × 3 (quick), 4 × 4, or 5 × 5 (free space in the middle).
   - **Token:** an animal, a shape in any color, or a nature or fun picture.
   Each card is dealt at random from about 40 strategies per deck, so it's different every time.
3. **Pop the bubble.** The **gold die** is the row and the **blue die** is the column. That square lights up and its card opens.
   - If the square is already covered, the student picks any open square in that row or column. If both are full, pop again.
   - You can also click any square to open it.
4. **Show it.** The card has a picture, simple steps, a **Read aloud** button, and for many strategies a breathing guide, hold timer or counter. When the student shows the strategy, click **They did it!** and the token drops onto the square.
5. **Bingo!** Any row, column, diagonal or the four corners wins (four corners doesn't count on 3 × 3). The student picks **1 new accessory** per bingo and **3** for a blackout. Accessories stay with the student, and they can change what their buddy wears any time with **Dress up**.

There are 26 accessories: hats, glasses, things for the neck, capes and wings, things to hold, and backgrounds.

## Strategies

Every strategy is tagged ⚡ **Wake up**, 🌙 **Calm down**, or both.

- **Move & Stretch** includes the Forest Animals yoga poses (Porcupine, Bear, Owl, Rabbit, Frog, Snake, Chipmunk, Woodpecker in a Tree, Skunk, Raccoon), more animal poses (Flamingo, Cat and Cow, Puppy Dog, Butterfly, Turtle Shell, Lion's Breath, Crab Walk and more), and movement such as wall push-ups, jumping jacks and cross crawls.
- **Calm & Think** includes breathing (Smell the Flower, Balloon Belly, Square Breathing, Bumble Bee Hum and more), grounding (5-4-3-2-1, Listen for 3 Sounds), and thinking tools: Size of the Problem, Name It to Tame It, End of the Rainbow, What's Ahead, Make a Plan, Kind Words to Myself and more.

**Strategies** on the home screen lets you:
- **Star** a strategy for a student, so it's always on their card (good for IEP goals or favorites).
- **Edit** the name, steps or wake-up/calm-down tag. Edits are saved in this browser. **End of the Rainbow** and **What's Ahead** have placeholder steps for you to change.

To change the strategies for everyone, edit `STRATS` near the top of the script in `index.html`. The pose drawings are in `POSE`.

The pose pictures are original drawings. The Forest Animals pose names come from a Tools to Grow card set; their pictures are not copied here.

Turn sound effects off with **Sound** at the top.
